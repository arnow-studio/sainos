À propos

Sainos, an anagram of the French word “saison” (season), is an independent project that champions short supply chains and local produce, to bring freshness and the rhythm of the seasons back to our plates.

Sainos is a magic word: mix up its letters and you get “saison”, French for season! Here you discover the fruit and vegetables that grow near you, so you can eat super fresh at the right time of year.

An educational goal

Our goal is purely educational: to give you the keys to a more responsible and tastier diet.

For learning

Discover nature while having fun: trick questions, cooking challenges and badges to collect.

Nothing to sell

There is no commercial aim behind Sainos: no sales, no advertising, no partnerships.

Nothing to sell

No ads here and nothing to buy. Just things to learn and enjoy.

Your privacy

No cookies are used to track you or collect your data. You can browse with complete peace of mind!

We don’t follow you

No cookies: nobody watches what you do on Sainos.

Why Sainos

It all started with a simple question in front of a market stall in the middle of winter: why strawberries in January? Looking for the answer, I discovered how much we don’t know about what we eat. Sainos brings together what I wish I had known sooner.

🔎

Where the information comes from

Season dates are cross-checked with the calendars of the French agencies ADEME, de Manger Bouger (Santé publique France) and Interfel, the French fresh produce industry body. Organic labels follow the Agence Bio, and the markets come from OpenStreetMap, the collaborative map. Spotted a mistake? Report it and it will be corrected.

✍️

Who’s behind it

My name is Arnaud. I create and update Sainos on my own, in my spare time. No company, no team, no investors: just the wish to share.

🗓️
