Banana

Harvested all year round in the French Caribbean, with no real season.

  • Season: all year round
  • Botanical family: Musaceae

History

Native to South-East Asia and New Guinea, the banana travelled to Africa, then to the Caribbean in the 16th century with Portuguese and Spanish sailors. It became a common fruit in France in the early 20th century, thanks to refrigerated ships.

Origin and growing regions

French bananas are grown all year round in the French Caribbean:

  • Guadeloupe
  • Martinique

Bananas from the French Caribbean are grown to some of the strictest pesticide standards in the world.

Benefits

The banana is the energy fruit:

  • Potassium and magnesium: They support healthy muscle function.
  • Vitamin B6: Present in good amounts.
  • Carbohydrates: About 90 kcal per 100 g, ideal for athletes.
  • Fibre: More of it when the banana is less ripe.

Varieties

  • Cavendish: The world’s best seller.
  • Plantain: Eaten cooked, like a vegetable.
  • Frécinette: Small, apple taste.
  • Red banana: Red skin, creamy flesh.

Zero waste

  • Storing: At room temperature, away from other fruit, never in the fridge: 4 to 6 days.
  • Freezing: Peeled, sliced: up to 6 months, ideal for smoothies and ice cream.
  • Nothing goes to waste: Black bananas are the best for banana bread, pancakes or “nice cream”.
