A free classroom resource

Sainos in class

Recognising fruit and vegetables, understanding the seasons, knowing where our food comes from: Sainos offers illustrated guides, quizzes by age and a calendar, ready to use as they are in class.

Lesson ideas by level

Simple ideas, free to adapt. Remember to switch on kids mode (top right): the texts are then written for 8 to 12-year-olds.

🐣 Nursery & receptionAges 4-6

Guess the fruit

On the whiteboard, as a whole class: the “Ages 4-6” quiz shows a photo and the class votes. Follow-up: bring in the real fruit to touch, smell and taste.

Ages 4-6 quiz →
🌱 CP – CE2Ages 7-9

The class calendar

At the start of each month, the class picks three seasonal products from the calendar and puts them on display. At the end of the month: the “Ages 7-9” quiz to check what everyone remembers.

Calendar →Ages 7-9 quiz →
🌳 CM1 – CM2Ages 10-12

Where does my plate come from?

In groups, each pupil investigates a product using its guide and the map of origins: season, region, distance travelled. Then they present it orally.

Map of origins →Guides →
🏅 Secondary schoolAges 11-15

Organic, local or seasonal?

A structured debate based on the comparison on the Organic page, then a team challenge with the “Surprise challenge” quiz and the plant families in the glossary.

Organic page →Glossary →
