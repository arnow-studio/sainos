Camembert de Normandie

Soft, creamy paste with intense notes of undergrowth, mushroom and warm butter.

  • Milk: Cow
  • Family: Bloomy rind
  • Origin: Normandy, France
  • Best season: April – October

Ripening

Ripened for at least 21 days and turned regularly.

What to serve it with

Fresh baguette and apple slices
