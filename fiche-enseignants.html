Seasonal fruit and vegetables in the classroom

Sainos is an independent educational website that teaches children to recognise fruit and vegetables, understand the seasons and discover where their food comes from.

🔓No accountNo sign-up, for pupils or teachers.
🛡️Zero dataNo cookies, no data collection. GDPR-compliant.
🚫No advertisingNothing to sell, no commercial partners.
🖥️AnywhereWhiteboard, tablet, computer, phone.

What pupils will find

44 illustrated guides : history, season, where it grows, benefits, cooking challenge.
A quiz for each age : ages 4-6, 7-9, 10-12 and secondary, 10 questions per game.
A seasonal calendar month by month.
A map of origins : where our fruit and vegetables come from.
A glossary : drupe, tuber, PDO… explained simply.
A kids mode with texts written for ages 8-12, and badges.

Links with the French curriculum

Cycle 1 (ages 3-6)Exploring the living world: naming, recognising, tasting.
Cycle 2 (ages 6-9)Understanding the world: living things, the passing of time, the seasons.
Cycle 3 (ages 9-12)Science: diet, where food comes from. Geography: consumption.
SustainabilityResponsible consumption, short supply chains, reducing food waste.
